My Cat Robot

a small project, one page

What is it?

My cat robot is a small wheeled robot that looks like a cat and acts like one, too. It rolls around the room, watches what I do, and occasionally pretends to be asleep on my desk.

What can it do?

  • Drive forward and turn on two little wheels
  • Blink its eyes when it is idle
  • Stop when it gets too close to a wall

How it works

  • A battery powers everything
  • A small microcontroller runs the logic
  • A distance sensor keeps it away from walls
